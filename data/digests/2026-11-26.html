Clinical trials

Faron Pharmaceuticals (FARN). The same Faron Pharmaceuticals trial (“A Study to Assess Safety, Tolerability and Preliminary Efficacy of Bexmarilimab in…”) is registered in two places, and they no longer agree: ClinicalTrials.gov still lists it as active, not recruiting, while EU CTIS shows it as ended. Usually one record just hasn't been updated yet, but a gap like this can be the first public sign that a trial has wound down.
