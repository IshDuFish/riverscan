What RiverScan is

RiverScan reports changes in public records, such as clinical trial registries, and links them to listed companies. We describe what changed. We do not tell you what it means for any share price.

Accuracy

Public registries can be incomplete, out of date or wrong, and our matching of trials to companies can contain errors. We try hard to be accurate but cannot guarantee that any information is complete, correct or current. Always check the original source.
