Data

Download the data.

Everything on the site comes from a few public files that update every morning after the scan. Download them, or point your own code at them.

Files

FileWhat's in it
signals.jsonJSONEvery signal ever logged, oldest first. Nothing is edited after the fact; new signals are added to the end.
signals.csvCSVThe same signals as a spreadsheet, with the main columns.
coverage.jsonJSONWhich companies are covered today, trial counts by registry, and which companies were left out and why (for example, worth over £1bn).
mismatches.jsonJSONTrials where the registries currently disagree about whether the trial is still running.
track_record.jsonJSONProgress of the price-reaction study. Results are added once enough signals have a full price history.

Using it in code

The files are plain static files, so any language can read them. In Python:

import pandas as pd

signals = pd.read_json("https://riverscan.com/data/signals.json")
recent = signals[signals.severity != "low"].tail(20)
print(recent[["detected_at", "company", "note"]])

Or straight into a spreadsheet: in Excel, Data → From Web, or in Google Sheets, =IMPORTDATA("https://riverscan.com/data/signals.csv").

Fields in a signal

detected_atWhen RiverScan caught the change (UTC, ISO 8601). The scanner runs at about 06:17 UK time.
streamtrials or contracts.
sourceWhere the change appeared: ClinicalTrials.gov, ISRCTN, EU CTIS, Find a Tender or Contracts Finder. Registry mismatches name both, e.g. ClinicalTrials.gov vs EU CTIS.
ticker, companyThe London-listed company and its ticker.
refThe record's own ID: an NCT number, ISRCTN number, EU CT number or contract notice ID.
titleThe trial or contract title, as published.
typechange, new_trial, removed, mismatch or contract_award.
fieldFor changes, which field moved, e.g. status, primary_completion, enrollment.
old, newThe value before and after. For contract awards, new is the contract value.
severityhigh, medium or low. See how to read a signal.
noteA short description of the change.
urlLink to the original public record.
publishedWhen the record itself says it changed or was published, where available.
buyer, framework, years, share_of_revenueContract awards only: who awarded it, whether it's a shared framework, its length in years, and its value per year as a share of the winner's annual revenue.

Updates

The scan runs every morning at about 06:17 UK time, before the London market opens, and the files update a few minutes later. Field names are kept stable; if one ever has to change, it will be announced here first.
