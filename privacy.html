Legal

Privacy notice.

What we collect, why, and your rights.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who we are

RiverScan (riverscan.com) is run by Ishan, based in London, United Kingdom. For anything about your data, use our contact form or reply to any RiverScan email.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the early access list.
  • The date and time you signed up, so we have a record of your consent.
  • Anything you send through the contact form: your email address and message, so we can reply.

We don't use advertising trackers. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads. Our host, GitHub Pages, keeps standard server logs.

Why we use it

We use your email only to send you RiverScan updates and, the weekly signals digest. Our legal basis is your consent, which you give by submitting the form.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. Sign-ups and contact messages are collected by our form provider, Formspree, and the website is hosted on GitHub Pages. If we start using an email service to send updates, we will list it here.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent at any time. Just get in touch through the contact form. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
