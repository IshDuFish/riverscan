Questions

What is RiverScan?

RiverScan is a data analytics company. We collect public records every day, find what changed, link each change to the London-listed company it affects, and publish the result as signals. We don't give investment advice.

Why only companies under £1bn?

Large companies are followed by dozens of analysts, so news about them is priced in quickly. Smaller companies get far less attention, which is where public records are most likely to tell you something the market hasn't noticed yet. The trade-off is that smaller shares can be harder and more expensive to buy and sell.

Is this investment advice?

No. RiverScan reports changes in public records. We never tell you to buy or sell anything, and nothing here is a personal recommendation. See our terms and disclaimer.

Where does the data come from?

Clinical trials come from ClinicalTrials.gov (US National Library of Medicine), ISRCTN (the UK registry) and EU CTIS (European Medicines Agency). Government contracts come from Find a Tender and Contracts Finder, the UK government's official procurement services. Every signal links back to the original record.

What's a registry mismatch?

The same trial is often registered in more than one place. When one registry says it's still running and another says it has ended, we flag it. Often one record is just out of date, but sometimes it's the first place a change shows up.

How do you decide how important a contract is?

By how big it is for the company that won it. We take the contract's value per year and compare it with the company's annual revenue: 10% or more is high, 2% or more is medium. A £5m contract can matter more to a £50m company than £300m does to a giant. A framework place means the company is one of several approved suppliers sharing a headline value that may never be fully spent, so we assume an equal share and never rate it high.

How quickly do signals appear?

The scanner runs every morning before the London market opens. Changes appear on the signal log straight away, and the most important ones go into the Friday email.

What happens to my email?

We use it only to send you RiverScan updates. You can unsubscribe any time. Read the privacy notice.
